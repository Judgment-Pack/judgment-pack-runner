Runner v0.21.0: an input profile may hold its page receipts to an endpoint scope instead of one exact endpoint (RFC 0018 round 15), so one reviewed page profile verifies a research source's reads of more than one URL; `verify-run` names `profile-mismatch` when a release's frozen profile is not among the profiles it is given.

## What an operator must do

- **Nothing for existing profiles and runs.** A profile without `endpointScope` keeps its form, its digest and its exact endpoint check; exports, the journal and the forty-one earlier vectors are byte-identical.
- **To let one page profile verify several URLs**, register it with `endpoint` `null` and `endpointScope`, an array of 1 to 32 prefixes in the constrained page form (`https://`, a DNS name, nothing, `:443` or `:` for the port, an optional path), or `https://*` on a profile of class `generated`. The profile must be of shape `http` with the adapter `adapter-web`, and a `generated` one declares `research` with the capability `page` alone. It binds to a research source's `page` assignment; bound to an operation or a selected file it is `profile-mismatch`.
- **If you acquire pages for a research source**, hold every requested URL, discovered ones included, to the scope before sending it, as `docs/MAPPING-V2.md` ("Admission before acquisition is the caller's") states; the Runner itself sends no page request, and refuses a receipt outside the scope at verification. (These notes first said the Desk already does so; it does not yet, judgment-pack-desk#423.)
- **If you read `verify-run`'s refusal of a release whose frozen profile is not among those given**, it now ends "(profile-mismatch). No decision was requested." as other coded refusals do; the exit code is unchanged.

## An endpoint scope (#142, closes #141)

- `endpointScope` is admitted on every path that takes profiles (start, `POST /v1/input-profiles`, `verify-run --profiles`, and the offline `verify-preparation`, digested over canon bytes), with RFC 0018 §5.1's refusals: `input-over-bound` for its bounds, counted first; `input-malformed` for its form, the shape and adapter, a non-null `endpoint`, or the wildcard on a `record` profile.
- Check 3 under a scope: the receipt's `acquisition.endpoint`, the final URL after followed redirects with its query removed, must be in the constrained form and match an entry (hosts equal with ASCII case folded, the three spellings of port 443 equal, the path a prefix at a literal `/` boundary, no decoding). Without a scope the comparison is byte for byte as before. One matcher, written from the text, serves every path.
- Six new preparation vectors record judgment-pack-spec#199's evidence over synthetic pages a stand-in served: two paths of one origin and two origins under the wildcard, read and verified; an endpoint outside the scope, a redirect target outside it and a changed request, each `acquisition-invalid`; a changed scope, `profile-mismatch`. The specification stages them (judgment-pack-spec#208).
