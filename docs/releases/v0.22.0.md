Runner v0.22.0: a profile's endpoint scope follows RFC 0018 round 16, refusing escaped slashes and backslashes and dot segments carrying parameters in a scoped page path; a refused stored profile is named when it stops the store from opening; a private offline consistency reader for retained draft workflows lands without any exposed surface.

## What an operator must do

- **Nothing for exact or unscoped profiles.** Their check is unchanged, and every published vector is byte-identical to v0.21.0's.
- **If you registered or configured a scoped page profile under v0.21.0**, check each `endpointScope` entry first. An entry v0.21.0 admitted that now carries an escaped slash or backslash (`%2f`, `%2F`, `%5c`, `%5C`), or a segment whose part before its first `;`, `%3b`, `%3f`, `%23` or `%00` is a dot segment, is `input-malformed`, and the Runner refuses to open its store, naming the profile (`registered input profile "<id>" is refused: …`). Registrations are immutable; how to recover such a store is open as #154. A page whose path carries an escaped slash can still be read under an exact-endpoint profile naming that URL.
- **If you acquire pages for a research source under a scope**, hold every requested URL to round 16's form before sending it; a receipt outside it is refused at verification.

## Round 16's page path form (#153, closes #152)

- A scoped page path carries no escaped slash or backslash anywhere, and no segment's part before its first `;`, `%3b`, `%3B`, `%3f`, `%3F`, `%23` or `%00`, whichever comes first, is a dot segment; nothing is decoded. One form serves scope entries on the live and offline paths and check 3 on each receipt, as RFC 0018 §5.1 at judgment-pack-spec `4bec33a` states it.
- The Runner and the specification's Python reader answer all 152 of the specification's endpoint-scope controls, round 15's and round 16's, byte for byte alike; a Runner built from v0.21.0 differs on exactly the 46 new refusals.
- A configured or registered profile the form refuses now stops the store from opening with the profile's id and its refusal, where the error named nothing.

## A private offline reader for retained draft workflows (#144, part of #121)

- Checks a retained draft RFC 0020 workflow bundle's consistency across the whole current branch against independently supplied workflow, execution and Runtime pins and historical profiles. Private: no API, command or conformance claim. A cross-vendor audit after its merge (recorded on #144) found most of its consistency checks untested and two rules in tension; those are open as #148–#151 and judgment-pack-spec#213.
